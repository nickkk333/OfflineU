How to Use

  • Prepare your course files in a directory structure
  • Browse to the course folder (or paste its path above)
  • Load it and start learning - progress is saved automatically
  • Come back anytime - your courses are remembered and the last one reopens even after a restart
  • Continuous playback - a finished video rolls into the next one and your playback speed is kept
  • Use ← / → to skip 10 s, space to pause, ↑ / ↓ for volume

Supported File Types

  • Videos: .mp4, .mkv, .avi, .mov, .webm
  • Audio: .mp3, .wav, .m4a, .aac
  • Documents: .txt, .md, .html, .pdf
  • Subtitles: .srt, .vtt (converted to WebVTT on the fly)
  • Quizzes: any document whose name contains "quiz", "exam" or "test"
